FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Didn’t find what you were looking for? Get in touch — we respond as soon as possible.

Ask a question
What exactly can Resolvo help me with?

Resolvo handles a wide range of personal and business responsibilities, including organising appointments, coordinating logistics, managing errands, researching solutions, communicating with service providers, and overseeing everyday tasks or special projects. If something requires your time, attention or coordination, there’s a good chance we can take care of it.

How does your service work, and how much does it cost?

We begin by understanding your needs, priorities and expectations. Based on these, we recommend a personalised service package that provides the appropriate level of support. Our services are primarily designed around ongoing partnerships, with transparent pricing and a clearly defined scope of responsibilities. Plans start at €790 per month.

Why should I trust Resolvo with my personal or business responsibilities?

Trust, discretion and accountability are at the core of Resolvo. We deliberately work with a limited number of clients to ensure dedicated attention, direct communication and consistent service quality. Our goal isn’t simply to complete individual tasks, but to become a dependable partner you can confidently delegate responsibilities to.

Do you take urgent or after-hours requests?

Yes. With Resolvo Signature we are on retainer 24/7, and urgent requests are always taken seriously.

Which area do you cover?

Primarily Novi Sad. By arrangement, service extends to the rest of Vojvodina and to Belgrade.

What if what I need isn’t on your list?

Ask anyway. If we don’t do it ourselves, we know someone who does — or someone who knows someone.

How do I pay?

Monthly, by bank transfer or in cash.
